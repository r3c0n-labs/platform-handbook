# On-call

One week at a time, Monday to Monday, handover at 10:00. The rota lives in
the on-call calendar; swap with someone and update it yourself.

## Handover

The outgoing engineer writes three lines in the team channel: what paged,
what's still open, and anything that might page this week.

## Escalation

1. The on-call engineer, paged by the alerting tool.
2. After 15 minutes without an acknowledgement, the secondary.
3. After 30, the platform lead.

Being paged at night earns the next morning off.
