# Incidents

1. Declare it in the incident channel, even if you're not sure.
2. One person leads, one person writes things down.
3. Fix first, find the cause after.
4. A write-up within five working days, blameless.

## Telling people

Post an update in the incident channel every 30 minutes, even when nothing
changed. Use this shape:

```text
Status:   investigating | mitigated | resolved
Impact:   who can't do what
Next:     what we're trying, and when the next update is
```
