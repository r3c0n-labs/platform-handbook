# Onboarding

Welcome to the platform team. Your first week is about access and context,
not tickets.

## First-week checklist

- [ ] Laptop enrolled and single sign-on working.
- [ ] Added to the platform team's chat channel and the on-call calendar.
- [ ] Read access to the infrastructure repositories.
- [ ] A walkthrough of the deploy runbook with your buddy.
- [ ] One on-call handover shadowed before you join the rota.
- [ ] The last three incident write-ups read.

## Who to ask

Your buddy first, then the team channel. Nobody minds a question that saves
an outage.
